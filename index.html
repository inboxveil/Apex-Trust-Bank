FAQs

Common Questions,
Answered Directly

Have something specific in mind? Start with these frequently asked questions.

How do I open an account online?

Complete the application. A customer profile and account number are created, with administrator approval required before dashboard access.

Is this a live bank?

No. This is a website prototype. It does not accept deposits, hold funds, provide licensed banking services, or process real financial transactions.

How do I contact support?

Use the Deposit Support button in your customer dashboard to open the Tawk.to chat widget.

How do I reset my password?

Use the password reset option. The Firebase account uses a generated account-number login ID; the administrator should assist with account recovery until a secure recovery flow is configured.
