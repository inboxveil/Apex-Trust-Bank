FAQs

Common Questions,
Answered Directly

Have something specific in mind? Start with these frequently asked questions.

How do I open an account online?

Choose Open Account and complete the four-step application. Your Firebase account and customer profile will be created, with approval required before customer access.

Is my money safe with Apex Trust Bank?

This website is not a licensed banking service and does not hold deposits or process real financial transactions. Do not deposit money or submit sensitive financial information for banking purposes.

Can I transfer money internationally?

International transfers are not enabled. A secure, authorized banking provider and backend would be required.

How do I reset my password?

Use the Forgot Password link on the sign-in page. Firebase will send a password reset email to the address registered to your account.

Can I open a business account?

The registration form includes personal and business account types. Actual banking services require appropriate authorizations and partners.
